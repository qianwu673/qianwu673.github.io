Qian Wu

Associate Professor, Ph.D.
Research Center for Computing and Multimedia Studies , Hosei University

I work in the field of Operations Research, with a particular focus on network optimization. My current academic interests have expanded toward the ethical use and governance of large language models, as well as the broader impact of AI on higher education. I am also interested in philosophy, especially where philosophical questions intersect with technology, education, and human agency.
